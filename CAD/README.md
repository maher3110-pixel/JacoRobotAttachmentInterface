# CAD Files

This folder contains the official interface designs and the research and development files created during the design process.

## Official Study Interfaces

The Official Study Interfaces folders contain the final official versions of the interface. The Fusion 360 folder contains the editable `.f3d` designs, and the STEP folder contains the same two designs in `.step` format.

## Research & Development

The Research & Development folders contain supporting models and the design work leading to the final versions.

- **Gripper:** JACO models provided by Kinova.
- **Last Year Assets:** Designs made by Adam Green, the student who worked on this project before me.
- **Online Attachment Models:** Models collected while exploring attachment methods.
- **Palm Designs and Tool Designs:** Iterations from the design process leading to the final versions.
- **Holster Designs:** Holster design files from the research and development work.

The STL folder contains five gripper mesh files.
